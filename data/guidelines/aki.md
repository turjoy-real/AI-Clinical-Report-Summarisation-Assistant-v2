# Acute Kidney Injury (AKI): Teaching Card
Topic: aki
Audience: student demo
Citation: KDIGO, NIDDK

This card covers AKI recognition and initial approach: rising creatinine, reduced urine output, and attention to volume status, nephrotoxins, and obstruction. Emphasize serial monitoring and identifying reversible causes.

Teach avoidance of nephrotoxins when possible and the role of fluid optimization. This is educational material; consult local guidelines for management details.

Suggested retrieval queries: AKI causes, nephrotoxins list, AKI initial evaluation

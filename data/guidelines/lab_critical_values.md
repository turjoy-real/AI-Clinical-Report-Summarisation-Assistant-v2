# Laboratory Critical Values: Teaching Overview
Topic: lab_critical_values
Audience: student demo
Citation: MedlinePlus

This card lists common critical lab value concepts: severe hyperkalemia, critical hypoglycemia, critically high troponin in context, and extreme acid-base derangements. Emphasize recognizing patterns and urgent notification policies; actual thresholds vary by laboratory.

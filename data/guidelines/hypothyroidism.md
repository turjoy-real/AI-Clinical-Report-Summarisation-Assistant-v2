# Hypothyroidism: Essentials for Students
Topic: hypothyroidism
Audience: student demo
Citation: MedlinePlus, NIDDK

This card covers primary hypothyroidism signs (fatigue, weight gain, cold intolerance), diagnostic approach with TSH and free T4, and basic replacement concepts. Emphasize individualized targets and monitoring of symptoms and labs.

Suggested retrieval queries: hypothyroidism diagnosis, TSH/free T4 interpretation, levothyroxine monitoring

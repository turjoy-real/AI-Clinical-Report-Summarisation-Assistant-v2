# Anemia: Approach to Diagnosis
Topic: anemia
Audience: student demo
Citation: MedlinePlus, WHO

This card outlines a simple framework for anemia: confirm low hemoglobin, classify by mean corpuscular volume (MCV), and consider iron studies, B12/folate, and reticulocyte count. Emphasize common causes like iron deficiency and chronic disease for learners.

Teach that severity, chronicity, and clinical context guide urgency and further testing. Avoid patient-specific recommendations; focus on differential diagnosis and initial labs.

Suggested retrieval queries: anemia workup, MCV classification, iron deficiency testing

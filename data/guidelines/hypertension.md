# Hypertension: Basics for Learners
Topic: hypertension
Audience: student demo
Citation: MedlinePlus, CDC

This card summarizes blood pressure categories, initial evaluation, and lifestyle measures. Teach that normal blood pressure is generally <120/80 mmHg. Elevated and stage classifications help guide counseling and treatment thresholds per public teaching sources.

Initial evaluation emphasizes repeat measurements, home or ambulatory monitoring when available, and assessment for secondary causes. Lifestyle changes include sodium reduction, weight loss, activity, and moderation of alcohol intake.

Suggested retrieval queries: blood pressure categories, lifestyle hypertension, secondary causes

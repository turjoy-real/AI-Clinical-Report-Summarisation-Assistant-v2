# Diabetes: HbA1c and Management
Topic: diabetes
Audience: student demo
Citation: MedlinePlus, WHO

This card covers HbA1c teaching ranges, common first-line therapy, and follow-up plans for learners. HbA1c reflects average blood glucose over ~3 months. Approximate teaching ranges: normal <5.7%, prediabetes 5.7–6.4%, diabetes ≥6.5%.

Metformin is commonly recommended as first-line oral therapy for type 2 diabetes in many public teaching sources; it is generally dosed cautiously when estimated glomerular filtration rate (eGFR) is reduced. Avoid starting or continue with caution when kidney function is severely impaired — consult local guidance.

Suggested follow-up: after initiating or changing therapy, recheck HbA1c in 3 months; if stable, every 3–6 months. Emphasize lifestyle, individualized targets, and safety monitoring for side effects.

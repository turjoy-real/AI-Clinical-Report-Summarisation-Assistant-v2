# Heart Failure: Core Teaching Points
Topic: heart_failure
Audience: student demo
Citation: MedlinePlus, AHA

This card summarizes heart failure phenotypes (reduced vs preserved ejection fraction), common symptoms (dyspnea, fatigue, fluid overload), and basic management principles such as salt/fluid management and medication classes used in chronic therapy.

Teach students to recognize decompensation signs and the importance of volume assessment. This is educational content only, not prescriptive clinical guidance.

Suggested retrieval queries: heart failure HFrEF vs HFpEF, decompensated heart failure signs, fluid management

# Acute Coronary Syndrome (ACS): Learner Summary
Topic: acs
Audience: student demo
Citation: MedlinePlus, AHA

This card introduces ACS presentations: chest pain, diaphoresis, dyspnea, and variable ECG changes. Teach the concept of early risk stratification and the need for urgent evaluation when ACS is suspected.

Students should learn common diagnostic tools (ECG, troponin) and that time-critical pathways exist in acute care. This is educational only.

Suggested retrieval queries: ACS presentation, ECG STEMI, troponin basics

# Sepsis: Early Recognition (Educational)
Topic: sepsis
Audience: student demo
Citation: WHO, CDC

This card focuses on early recognition signs of sepsis for learners: suspected infection plus systemic signs such as altered mental status, tachypnea, hypotension, or high lactate. Emphasize the importance of timely assessment and source control in teaching materials.

Teach basic initial steps: urgent assessment, blood cultures before antibiotics when feasible, and early administration of appropriate antimicrobials guided by clinical judgment and local protocols.

Suggested retrieval queries: sepsis early signs, sepsis initial management, source control teaching
